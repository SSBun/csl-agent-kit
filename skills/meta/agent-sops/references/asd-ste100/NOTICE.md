The files were copied from the user's checked local conversions. The rule text and dictionary copy are unchanged. The dictionary has no source positions, page labels, record IDs, or document metadata.

The dictionary keeps seven fields: `headword`, `part_of_speech`, `approved`, `word_and_forms`, `meaning_or_alternatives`, `ste_examples`, and `non_ste_examples`.

## Conversion Limits

The source introduction states 875 approved words and 1,274 non-approved words. The extracted body contains 879 approved records and 1,319 non-approved records. The conversion keeps all body records. It does not remove records to match the introductory totals or infer the cause of the difference.

The phrases `FOR EXAMPLE` and `such as` have no printed part-of-speech label. Their `part_of_speech` value is `null`. The `re-` prefix has no source examples. Empty example fields preserve that fact.

Text comparison covered the rule sections and dictionary records. It ignored whitespace differences. The conversion is not a claim of full manual proofreading or a certified edition. Use the official source when an entry or layout is unclear.
